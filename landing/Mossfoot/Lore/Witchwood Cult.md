## What's known

The cult takes recruits from travellers along the Witchwood Road, usually the kind no one will miss. They wear bone-bleached masks during their ceremonies and never seem to leave tracks.

> [!dm] DM only
> They're a splinter of the older Order of the Pale Crown. Their goal is to reawaken something buried under the [[Hidden Caves]]. The masks are stitched from finger bones. One per recruit, taken before initiation.
>
> Their founding text, "On Buried Things", was written by a younger [[Dr. Bixby Wizzlethorpe]] as an academic curiosity. He has no idea they exist.

## Confirmed casualties

- Aelar's sister Liriel (3 winters ago).
- Two travelling merchants, summer last.
- A Balandel courier whose body was returned headless.
