A miniature sample campaign set around [[The Mossfoot Inn]], written exactly the way you'd write your own campaign vault. The [[Features]] pages point into this folder whenever a demo needs something real: the NPCs demo role gating and `foundry.source` clones, the Audio files demo passthroughs, the Macros demo handlers, and the whole folder syncs into Foundry VTT as journals, Actors, Items, a Scene, a card deck, a playlist, macros, and a rolltable (see [[Foundry integration]]).
